About Me.

THE STORY SO FAR

Hey, I’m Kartik Jaiswal, originally from Varanasi and currently based in Bangalore, pursuing my first year in Cyber Security at DSATM.

I’m someone who enjoys exploring how things work, solving challenging problems, and turning ideas into something real. Whether it’s working through complex math problems, playing chess, or experimenting with new technologies, I love activities that challenge the way I think.

Beyond technology, I have a strong creative side. I enjoy video editing with DaVinci Resolve, graphic design, and creating visuals that communicate ideas and tell stories.

I’m also interested in web development and app development, and I’m currently working on building an app of my own.

My curiosity goes beyond software. I’m fascinated by drones, Raspberry Pi, and hardware-software integration, especially the possibilities that emerge when different technologies work together. Cybersecurity is another area I’m exploring, and I’m excited to learn more about how systems work, how vulnerabilities are discovered, and how technology can be made more secure.

I believe the best way to learn is by building, experimenting, and sometimes figuring things out the hard way. I’m always looking for new skills to learn, interesting projects to work on, and opportunities to turn my curiosity into something meaningful.

For me technology is more than just code — it’s a way to create, experiment, and bring ideas to life.
