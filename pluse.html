01 · The SOS button

Press and hold for 2 seconds.

The big SOS button on the Alert screen is all you need. Hold it for two seconds and an emergency alert goes out to every saved contact, along with your live location.

  • Hold the SOS button for 2 seconds on the Alert screen.
  • Every saved contact gets your help message together with your live location.
  • The alert is logged in History with a SENT status.
Tap to sendLive locationAll contacts
02 · Shake to alert

No need to even open the app.

In an accident or any emergency, just shake your phone hard. The motion sensor triggers the alert automatically, and your saved contacts get your live location and message. It needs a strong shake, so it will not fire by mistake.

  • Works in the background, so the app does not need to be open.
  • A strong shake is needed, so it will not fire by mistake.
  • Turn Shake to Alert on or off and adjust sensitivity right on the Alert screen.
Works in the backgroundAdjustable sensitivityHands-free
03 · Phone switched off?

Powered off? The alert still goes.

If your phone gets switched off, Pulse automatically sends your live location and a help message to your emergency contacts. You do not have to do anything.

  • When the phone is switched off, Pulse sends the alert on its own.
  • Your live location and help message go to your emergency contacts automatically.
  • You do not have to unlock or tap anything.
Auto locationAuto help message
04 · Always ready

Turns on by itself. Barely touches your battery.

The moment your phone starts, Pulse starts quietly in the background. No need to open it again and again, and battery use stays very low.

  • Switch your phone on and Pulse starts quietly in the background by itself.
  • You never have to open the app again and again.
  • Battery use stays very low, so it runs all day without you noticing.
Auto start on bootUltra low battery
05 · Contacts and your own message

Your words. Your people.

Save a name, a primary number and an optional secondary number. Write a custom message in your own words. That exact message, with your live location, is what your emergency contacts receive.

  • Save a name, a primary number and an optional secondary number.
  • Write your own help message in your own words.
  • Your saved contacts are listed in the app and can be removed any time.
Custom messagePrimary + secondarySaved contacts
06 · Smart status alerts

Low battery or no internet? You'll see it right away.

If your battery drops below 20%, a warning appears at the top of the Alert screen. If the internet is off, you get a notice that alerts will go as a plain SMS with coordinates. You always know your phone is ready.

  • Battery below 20%: a low battery warning shows at the top of the Alert screen.
  • No internet: a notice shows that alerts will go as a plain SMS with coordinates.
  • You know your phone is ready before an emergency happens.
Battery below 20%No internet noticeAlways informed
07 · No internet? Still covered.

Offline alerts with coordinates.

If your net is off, Pulse attaches your last known location, or your GPS latitude and longitude, to a plain SMS and sends it. Help can still find you.

  • Pulse attaches your GPS latitude and longitude, or your last known location.
  • It goes as a plain SMS, so no mobile data is needed.
  • Your contacts get the coordinates and can find you.
Plain SMS fallbackLat / LongLast location
08 · History

See every alert, any time.

The History tab lists recent alerts: who was alerted, on what date and at what time.

  • Every alert shows who was alerted.
  • The exact date and time of each alert.
  • A SENT status so you know it went out.
